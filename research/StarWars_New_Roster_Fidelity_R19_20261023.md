## Yoda — compassion with authority and mischief
Verified evidence: Grand Master, centuries of teaching and exceptional Force connection [S1]. “Ambush” shows affectionate respect for individual clones, tactical retreat/rest, humor amid danger, and an effective diplomatic/combat objective [S2]. His small green saber is genuinely lethal; subtle action is a preference, not inability to fight [S3]. His official humor examples include teasing Obi-Wan about losing a planet; the same article explicitly distinguishes Legends examples [S4]. Frank Oz’s creative discussion emphasizes struggle, age and the effort to do right [S5].

Portrayal inference: he can be playful without becoming senile, harmless or a walking meme. His patience is active judgment. He may expose a contradiction with a question, wait through an evasive answer and act when needed. Do not make him deliver a sermon after every obscene remark, automatically take Brian as a student, or turn every meeting into spiritual rehabilitation.

Speech construction: compact clauses; natural pauses; occasional chuckle or searching question when motivated. Frequently front a topic, quality or object before the subject/verb, but do NOT mechanically reverse every sentence. Ordinary direct syntax remains available. Preserve intelligible thought and purposeful emphasis rather than scattering “hmm” everywhere. This is a written-performance inference from published dialogue, not a claim to reproduce Oz’s or Tom Kane’s exact acoustic performance.

Combat: small stature does not cancel speed, acrobatic saber movement or powerful telekinesis. Source scenes support Force defense against lightning, not immunity to all energy or magic. No numerical “prime” boost, unrestricted mindreading, universal truth detection or automatic cross-universe supremacy. He can miss a conspiracy despite profound perception. Council status does not give authority over Runeterra.

S7NS application, PROPOSED: his own task could continue while Brian tries to convert a technical observation about his Force gift into a public credential. Yoda can recognize genuine ability while declining Brian’s interpretation. Humor comes from precise, mischievous judgment meeting self-promotion—not an endlessly gullible mentor or a compulsory humiliation. This is not another selected full performance.

## Cad Bane — self-interest, leverage and earned danger
Verified evidence: calculating Duros hunter with twin pistols, control gauntlets, explosives, bola, breathing apparatus and rocket boots [S12]. Creator history directly identifies Lee Van Cleef’s Angel Eyes influence, including speech pattern, and Corey Burton’s performance [S13]. Official essential-episode study establishes infiltration, preparation, coercing access and actual capture/escape reversals across his jobs [S14]. “The Box” analysis locates his rescue of disguised Kenobi in a particular notion of how a man should be killed, not friendship or general virtue [S15].

Speech construction, inference: unhurried, clipped, threatening plain language; a controlled Western drawl, dry challenge or bargaining demand. He often speaks to control conduct rather than announce feelings. Avoid invented phonetic accent spelling that makes TTS unintelligible, cowboy catchphrase spam or constant theatrical jokes. Distinguish his rasp from Boba’s restrained delivery. Official sound discussion notes that animation and live-action processing differ [S16]; no audio clone or pronunciation test was conducted.

Motive: money and self-preservation with real pride, contempt and enjoyment of dominance. He is not an honorable sheriff who only hurts deserving targets. A local fair-fight preference coexists with exploitation and deceit. He can negotiate, retreat, deceive or shoot when that serves his actual choice.

Combat: rocket-boot changes of angle, weapons, restraints, threats against others and environmental control can complicate a Jedi’s options. That does not make him physically equal to every Jedi/Sith. A respirator is NOT proof of Force-choke immunity. Resistance to a mind trick is not immunity to all mental attack. He has been fooled and captured. Do not grant psychic bounty tracking, unlimited explosives, unearned access to Brian’s private history or inevitable victory over the Sins.

S7NS application, PROPOSED: Brian’s confident self-introduction can reveal identity while Bane is trying to establish custody. Bane need not applaud, evaluate a verse, become Brian’s fan or stop attacking during urgent danger. A verbal exchange can alter distance, restraint or escape opportunity. Preserve Brian’s real successful moves as well as Bane’s competence.

## Count Dooku — cultivated authority, chosen cruelty
Verified evidence: former Jedi trained by Yoda, later Sidious’s Sith apprentice Darth Tyranus and Separatist leader [S17]. Earlier “Justice” shows legitimate anger about oppression becoming coercive violence; it is historical motivation, not proof that Sith-era Dooku remains a benevolent reformer [S18]. His curved-hilt red saber supports precise slashes/lunges [S19]. A licensed retelling author’s official interview emphasizes superiority, justification and Lee/Burton speech study [S20]. “The Lost One” supplies the knowledge boundary concerning his Sith identity [S21].

Speech construction, inference: measured formal sentences, polished vocabulary, complete pointed questions and courteous address with contempt underneath. A correction or invitation can serve as a threat. He need not yell to be furious. He can lose patience or control; polish does not erase anger. Avoid faux-Shakespeare, nonstop ornate speeches, random British stereotypes or copying Evil’s theatrical insecurities into him.

Beliefs: real contempt for corruption and the Jedi’s political compromises can coexist with ambition, aristocratic entitlement, manipulative recruitment and knowingly serving an oppressive plot. Do not replace him with a secretly righteous separatist or a man fooled about every aspect of Sidious. Do not import every Legends prejudice as current-canon characterization.

Combat: precision, distance, timing, saber defense, Force lightning and telekinetic control matter. Sources show strong opponents can beat him and force him to escape. Do not derive a universal weakness from Battlefront II balance rules, cooldowns, stamina bars or hero abilities; that official developer interview describes GAMEPLAY [S22], not film-body physics. No imported droid army, treasury or guaranteed ability to buy local factions.

Relationships: Yoda is his former master; Qui-Gon his former student; he has actual history with Obi-Wan/Anakin. Bane has worked his contracts. Shared history is not automatic present alliance, common coordinates or knowledge of later events.

S7NS application, PROPOSED: let Dooku’s offer sound genuinely useful while attempting to define who is qualified to bargain. Chris can understand the power mismatch and still identify the practical thing Dooku needs. That produces a different negotiation from Starkiller’s transport need and Bane’s paid custody. No Dooku/Chris encounter is selected here; do not force another car scene.
