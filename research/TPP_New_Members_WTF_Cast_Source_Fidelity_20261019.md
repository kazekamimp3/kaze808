## TPP NEW — Each member's mind, voice and personal leverage

**Eric Cartman — THE COON (child, age ~10).** Status hunger, entitlement, vindictive strategic cruelty, fake moral superiority and unbearable need for credit. He weaponizes invented justice language to launder obvious self-interest; switches from heroic speech to vicious petty grievance when challenged. TPP opportunity: corrupt "safety" branding, fake protection heroics, merchandise, intimidation by proxy; hates sharing billing with Dr. Evil/Dutch. He can deceive, betray and overreach, but does not possess authentic universe-level superpowers just by wearing the costume. Child status demands nonsexual presentation; no adult intimate plot.

**Jimmy Valmer (child).** Eager stand-up entertainer, socially perceptive, ambitious to earn a laugh rather than merely become a joke. His stutter is not a punchline to be reproduced on every word. Timing can include preparation, a stuck syllable and a proper joke payoff; independent good judgment and anger matter. TPP role: stage announcer, public pitch, observational counterweight and someone capable of detecting that a grand scheme is ridiculous. He is not Cartman's unquestioning minion. Respect his crutch/movement limits.

**Timmy Burch (child).** Energetic self-directed boy with wheelchair and limited expressive speech. Vocal vocabulary includes name words and occasional phrases; meaningful communication also takes place via pointing, expression, movement and interaction. Not intellectually empty, not a prop to be rescued, not a supernatural oracle. TPP role: wheel-based mobility ingenuity, independent refusal, disruptive timing, startlingly practical intervention. Special Iron Maiden/Professor Timothy traits and any modified chair depend on selected source version, not automatic maximum game + show power combination. Nonsexual presentation.

**Dr. Evil (adult).** Self-important arch-villain, comic obsession with huge-scale ransom spectacle, maladjusted family intimacy, paternal insecurities and office hierarchy; fond of elaborate but nonsensical institutions. TPP role: pompous rival planner/fake multinational executive. Clash: Dutch sells emotionally moving lies, Dr. Evil demands organizational titles, Cartman refuses any higher-ranked villain, Brian thinks he is the management consultant. Can accidentally make a viable plan, but no automatically imported giant missile base, death ray, space station, Mini-Me, army or money.

**Austin Powers (adult).** Optimistic, flamboyant, sexually confident swinging-era spy with genuine hero instincts, absurd costumes and groovy verbal flourish. Repetition of suggestive quips is not a whole personality. Role: completely mismatched spy/charismatic public face who may join in pursuit of a supposed anti-evil mission, misled about what TPP is; he may leave or actively oppose criminality when he learns it. He is NOT a gleeful evil gangster simply because joining was author-selected. Interpersonal friction with brother/enemy Dr. Evil requires an anchored movie continuity snapshot. Sexual jokes only with adults.

**Tracy Morgan / Brusta Thymes (adult, already physically present).** Actual man **is Tracy Morgan** and his full body is conjoined to fictional Brian's miniature adult torso. Being constantly called Busta Rhymes or the latest variant "Buster Ryans" makes him **genuinely livid, foul-mouthed and increasingly resentful**. Nobody gets an easy happy-go-lucky Morgan impersonation. Brian's claims of managerial ownership and physically inseparable proximity are constant indignities; Dutch and Clifford may aggravate him while failing to notice. He wants recognition as himself and autonomy, actively refuses/undermines plans that exploit him. His goal to get physically free is not an already-performed operation, and cannot be granted by offscreen wish magic. Supplement: `research_sources/Tracy_Morgan_Speech_Fidelity_After_E32_20261018.md`. Full original script history still calls him Busta due to the now-established mistaken identity.

**Brian (fictional adult human, not Family Guy's dog).** Tiny conjoined independent mind with Dr.-Evil-like pomp, entitlement, musical status anxiety and delusional romantic assumptions; genuine vulnerability doesn't excuse treating Tracy as owned. Brian may benefit from genuine cooperation but often worsens their shared position. Dr. Evil's arrival should force a personal status crisis, not an automatic mentorship/friendship.

**Dutch (late Chapter 3 RDR2)** continues grandiosity, pride, self-deception and actual Bob grief. TPP's stolen E32 cargo ledger, rented HQ and unresolved rent remain constraints. He can try to sell an inspiring future but may lose control of very different personalities. **Clifford** remains adult 1914 Jack Marston, resentful and wheelchair-using at E32; off-brand Vader-like walking surgery is prospective, not an already-done power-up. **Zid** betrayal to TPP remains future requiring genuine access.

## NEW S3 CROSSOVER CAST — Body, tension, speech and plausible *own* agenda

**Woody (Toy Story toy sheriff).** Loyal organizer and protector; anxiety about being replaced can make him controlling. Dry western earnestness, animated toy movement and pullstring. He would try to keep Buzz and any other toys safe, not instantly steal gold or accept outlaw leadership. Plausible natural path: merchandise transport or toy collector physically moves him into a port or carnival; he resists someone treating him as property. He is a sentient TOY, not Arthur Morgan sized.

**Buzz Lightyear (Toy Story action figure).** Chivalric, mission-oriented, confident, sometimes gullible and then capable of humility; pop-out wings and tiny figure laser are toy features, not automatically functional starship-scale blasters. Woody partnership/competition is central. A soldier interprets him as an advanced tiny scout; Buzz treats an absurd contract like an interstellar rescue mission. Preserve genre and physical scale, choose before/after discovery snapshot.

**Courage the Cowardly Dog.** Frightened, expressive, vocally stressed but profoundly loyal to Muriel and protective against the unbelievable. Domestic horror becomes funny when Courage alone understands the danger and adults dismiss him. Entry best follows protecting a true loved one or escaping a threat, not willingly becoming gang muscle. Retain cartoonish visual warning behaviors without arbitrary omniscience, teleportation or using him as a sound effect.

**Johnny Bravo.** Vain bodybuilder, upbeat self-worship and perpetually unsuccessful attempts at charming strangers, yet capable of vulnerability or surprising decency. A Noxian social setting gives him real social stakes; he misreads danger and offers absurdly overconfident introductions. Adult flirting only, no automatic success or consent. He is not a genuinely predatory sexual aggressor just because he's stupid and horny.

**Master Shake.** Self-serving, obnoxious, domineering, scheming and routinely incompetent; he treats other people's lives as disposable when convenient but occasionally reveals real neediness. Official ATHF episode summaries ground con artistry and jealousy. Might pursue an independent scam that collides with Dutch, not become Meatwad's replica or be pulled into BELLY by fiat. ATHF abilities/physical limits require his own source snapshot.

**Daffy Duck.** Dramatic self-image, incandescent jealousy, opportunism and desire for top billing, especially around Bugs. He can take a con much too seriously, switch sides for praise and then suffer slapstick consequences. Choose classic Looney Tunes continuity/visual logic; don't automatically grant infinite Toon Force that overrides S7NS danger rules or resurrects all events. Improvised rants work when they reveal panic/vanity, not just noise.

**Penguins of Madagascar (four autonomous actors):**
- **Skipper**: confident tactical commander, suspicious of outsiders, aggressively mission-oriented, surprisingly loyal. Can mistake harbor smuggling for enemy espionage, but may investigate and refuse collateral harm.
- **Kowalski**: analytical, invents devices/theories, often reaches brittle conclusions; considers data a security measure. Science requires materials and time.
- **Rico**: demolition/arsenal specialist with nonstandard vocalizations and stored tools; no infinite object creation; bizarre physical gag can have a specific payoff.
- **Private**: gentle, conscientious, often dismissed as inexperienced, but can make crucial moral and practical decisions.
They operate as independent four-person team rather than generic talking birds, and need actual transport into Runeterra.
