## B. Film-to-film performed voice: specific mechanics
**2016 — The merc talking past his own hurt.** One dialogic thought starts with true affection or a practical job and is deliberately defaced by a grotesque bodily metaphor. Conversational pivot sequence: intimate reassurance -> improbable comparison -> a listener's naive question -> impossibly disgusting specificity -> immediate reorientation toward the real operation. Taxi chatter and the Colossus contrast make this audible. He often corrects official stories with his own selfish version, misrepresents motive, then accidentally reveals his pain. His image of himself is slippery and vulnerable, not an omnipotent stand-up god. The opening uses fractured chronology and a direct camera address to create a conspiratorial relationship with the audience, not to explain every action in advance.

**2018 — Framing lies and a human wound.** Opens by mocking the superhero/franchise formula and calls himself a family-film hero as carnage follows. The joke pays off as real loyalty to Russell and his makeshift family. Wade uses self-congratulatory heroic rhetoric, personalizing every moment, but his protection of a vulnerable kid becomes serious. His socially inappropriate observations are fast, startling and frequently both cruel and affectionate. His prolonged "dying" has childish audience hunger and real attachment in the same breath. His bad decisions cause harm and should remain consequential; regenerative ability isn't a comedy stop-clock for others. Family is a deep motive, not generic "never cares."

**2024 — Post-reunion source snapshot.** His self-worth is bruised by rejected heroism and estrangement; loneliness/mattering energizes showmanship. He can be confrontational over an absurdly specific piece of junk (the Honda Odyssey), treating it like a civilization-ending injustice, and he can stop mid-joke when someone he cares about might die. Wolverine's blistering minivan speech is the scene's emotional high point *because Wade actually goes silent*. He is simultaneously mean, performative, crass, attached, reactive and insecure. His fandom/meta comments are sharp but subject to a fourth-wall convention; they do not grant all timeline knowledge or the secrets of a Runeterran he hasn't met.
Selected S7NS pin remains **after 2024 apartment reunion**, not opening depressed salesman Wade. The E19 paid Noxian connection remains delivered but no repeated "first arrival."

## C. Speech engine to implement in S7NS narration
- **Audience complicity**: speaks to the listener as confidant/co-conspirator, teases their expectation and acknowledges things the narrative itself just demonstrated. Avoid direct self-report of confidential author prompts, tools, hidden manuscript or future canon.
- **Fast pivots with recoverable thread**: offer a concrete observation, escalate to utterly disproportionate vulgar comparison, undercut himself, return cleanly to whose action is changing the stakes. Don't wander so far that a TTS listener forgets who is where.
- **Inverted professionalism**: an absurdly polite announcement describes grotesque crime; a military funeral becomes an apology about how bad the seating is, then the grief is honored. The third beat changes the meaning of the first.
- **Inventive filth**: profanity is uncapped and flexible—crude anatomy, aggressively specific biological impossibilities, petty sexual innuendo among adults, bodily disgust, celebrity misrecognition, mocking bureaucracy, genre roast, death gallows humor. Don't paste the same set of "fuck, asshole, bitch" across every character.
- **Self-interruption and self-own**: fake a grand claim, get caught contradicting himself, acknowledge it without losing his place; relish being caught. A listener, physical incident or character may blow apart his intended punchline.
- **Meta calibration**: comic panels, soundtrack, casting, sequel/review/budget jokes permitted as *Deadpool's address to the audience*. Other characters need not recognize media reference and gain no meta awareness by osmosis. Keep jokes intelligible without fandom prerequisite.
- **Interpersonal edge**: insulting detail comes from the speaker's unique vanity, failure or terrible choice. Deadpool's narrator should hate Dutch's false inspiration, Brian's coercion, Zid's embarrassing incompetence and Tracy's captivity in different voices, not sneer generically at everyone.
- **Unpredictable relief**: trauma or cruelty can bring rare unvarnished sentences. The vulnerable silence that follows matters; no automatic quip at the climax. Wade can misjudge an enemy, be ashamed of a joke and revise his account.
- **Film specificity**: year-aware references to superheroes, action-film clichés, pop culture, comic books and Ryan's own franchise jokes are within the extradiegetic narrator layer; they do NOT retcon same-world awareness into Noxian townspeople.
- **Sound/TTS**: natural conversational contractions, varied sentence lengths, active verbs and coherent people/where/when orientation. Never reproduce Reynolds' recorded voice or require playback of a nonexistent audio performance. No 100-line rapid-fire dialogue ticker; clean paragraphs and timed interruptions.

## D. What to borrow from Rick Sanchez and what NOT to copy
**Borrow:** contempt for idiotic process, precise intellectual takedown, obscene cosmic perspective, pathological impatience, deranged analogy, furious riff that reveals a hidden operational flaw, flat dismissal of ceremonial authority and the ability to turn existential scale into a fight over something petty.
**Do NOT turn Wade into Rick:** No habitual drunken burps/stammers, grandfather/mentor posture, Rick's godlike gadget authority, self-satisfied certainty about all science or world rules, permanent emotional disconnection, habitual direct abuse of a "Morty" stand-in, Rick-specific catchphrases or spoken impersonation. Wade is a self-mocking mercenary who desperately wants chosen family. Rick's bitter brain is a **spice**. Rick himself remains a separate independent crossover actor and a nonmember of Sins unless author separately selects.
**Recommendation:** one recognizable Deadpool narrator with optional Rick-sharpened attack vectors, not two alternating narrator voices and not a featureless 50/50 hybrid.

## F. Scene-engine blueprint and original (not film) writing calibration
**Narration approach:** 
1. Establish place, actual event and physical stakes clearly.
2. Acknowledge the hideous/ironic fact with a very specific personal vulgar comparison, perhaps an annoying tangent.
3. Compare a participant's claim to observable reality; mock the self-serving contradiction with Rick-sharp surgical focus.
4. Let action or another character overturn Wade's expected joke. Avoid interrupting the significant beating, sacrifice or genuine grief.
5. Return to real consequences, grudges, resources and changed relationships in one clean turn.

That's not a gang; it's a sexually frustrated committee with a felony punch card. Anyway, the ledger mattered. Which is more than I can say for Dutch's last five fucking speeches."
