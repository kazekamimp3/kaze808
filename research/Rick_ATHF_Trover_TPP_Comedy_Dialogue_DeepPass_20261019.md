## Rick Sanchez: actual speech and social cognition

**Important finding:** The existing S7NS Rick research before this pass was NOT a complete speech-fidelity owner. Merely applying caustic, scientific or nihilistic prose to Deadpool misses the character's high-speed self-contradicting argumentative engine.

**Listening/reading patterns from the sampled episodes:**
1. **Condescension aimed at another person's premise:** Rick seldom says only "that's stupid." He rewrites what the other person is trying to prove, accuses it of being a childish fantasy, then provides an elaborate concrete absurd alternative. *Vat of Acid* is about Morty's critique of Rick's fake-acid plan getting under Rick's skin. Rick makes an outrageous fake "save" device not because it is necessary but because his superiority was challenged. The joke grows into monstrous consequences and the original petty insult still drives his obsession.
2. **Harsh "scientific" certainty that covers emotional avoidance:** In *Pickle Rick* his long anti-therapy reasoning sounds highly articulate and self-sufficient—until he admits he staged the pickle situation to avoid family therapy. Logic can be technically impressive and psychologically dishonest simultaneously. The therapist and daughter are permitted to be right.
3. **Repetition and repair as thought in motion:** quick broken starts, changing sentence subjects, sudden incredulous questions, half-reversed claims, escalating "well actually" proofs, unsolicited analogies. His verbal stammers, tempo and occasional burping are performance traits; TTS adaptation should use *clear audible segmentation*, not mechanically insert burps, random misspellings or indistinct word soup.
4. **Active grotesque reduction:** turns a child's hope, human love or ordinary dignity into chemical, transactional or animal logic while indirectly revealing a specific attachment he pretends not to have. Rick's existential conclusions are deployed in interpersonal fights, not merely philosophy quotes.
5. **Reality as an expensive answer to a cheap grievance:** *Meeseeks* gives finite-lifespan helper beings a simple golf task that turns into a horrific nightmare through Jerry's stubborn insecurity; *Rick Potion 9* compounds Morty's socially desperate request into a global biological catastrophe. These aren't random scene cuts: character flaws plus binding rules mutate the problem.
6. **Argument can invert genuine harm into useful knowledge:** In *Total Rickall* fake affectionate memories hide parasitic infestation, while ugly real memories help identify people who truly exist. The revelation is both nasty and truthful; the characters' painful relationships acquire a new function.
7. **Competence DOES NOT make him infallible:** his plan can succeed mechanically while harming everybody; his sneering can be wrong about moral cost; someone he undervalues can expose him. Repetitive unearned genius makes him boring.
8. **Character evolution matters:** Dan Harmon explicitly warns about flattening Rick into a repeated nihilism catchphrase. Rick's scarred personal psychology, family bonds and pride can contradict his ideology.

**Fusion to Deadpool narrator, as requested by the author:** *Wade remains the post-2024-film main voice*, while Rick contributes an EARNED, noticeable layer of fast adversarial reasoning, indignant logical dismantling, ego-defensive tangents, precision insults and sudden cosmic/petty register pivots. Rick's rhythm should influence how Wade interrogates a scene's claim—not simply sprinkling "science," "nihilism" or "fuck" into a paragraph. The two psyches DIFFER: Rick usually asserts inventor/control authority; Wade relies on personal embarrassment, film/industry awareness, shame, wounded relationships and audience complicity. The resulting narrator can try to be smarter than the scene, fail spectacularly, and admit it. No Rick co-narrator, voice identity merge, imported gadgets or physical actor knowledge.

**TTS calibration:** brief incredulous setup; tumbling argument and specific filthy analogy; rhetorical challenge; self-own; return to actual scene. Do NOT enforce fixed order or frequency. Example of originally drafted, NON-canon mechanism: "He claims the team has a plan. Sure. By that definition, swallowing your car keys is a transport strategy. Sorry, that's unfair—to car keys. Anyway, the woman across the road still has the only boat." This is original illustrative text, not a line copied from the series or an event in S7NS.

## Trover: bizarre individual reactions, not nonstop ad-lib noise

Primary producer description supports **improvised branching around a grounded event state** rather than spontaneous untraceable spectacle. Strangers with hideous bodies have petty personal rules; an almost understandable task receives a needlessly cruel social response; the next response remembers what the speaker just did. The comedy often feels like eavesdropping on people who believe their violently obscene universe is normal. Don't label every weird object a cosmic artifact or have everyone marvel in the same register.

**S7NS use:** let a BELLY resident's banal grievance about shared space collide with an actual digestive/prison/security reality; the incident changes their relationship or material option. Let contradictory voices cut off each other's riffs, with a core through-line and surprising alternate reactions. Editing should CUT weak improv, not preserve a 20-minute torrent just because its premises are outrageous.

## Aqua Teen: the three-person machinery, calibrated to multiple full scripts

**Shake is not merely a snarky furniture thief.** In *Rabbot*, he assumes command without competence, proposes spending nonexistent detective fees, abandons the genuine monster problem for his own appearance and invents justifications when challenged. In *Hands on a Hamburger*, he repeatedly and ingeniously manipulates rules to trick competitors out, threatens, flatters, cheats and pursues an outcome that is objectively stupid; spite drives nearly every stage. He is capable of inventive social sabotage; he need not be charming or secretly right.

**Frylock is NOT always the responsible parent.** He is a serious, often genuinely brilliant science-minded person whose inventions and pride can cause or worsen disasters. *Super Computer* starts with a revolutionary machine and becomes a grotesque time-travel/patent scam. *Total Re-Carl* begins with an environmentally friendly toilet and escalates into horrifying bodily consequences when scientific ambition overrides the other person's autonomy. *Super Trivia* demonstrates Frylock willing to alter his companions' knowledge/eyes to win a petty contest. He retains compassion and reason, but can rationalize highly intrusive science.

**Meatwad is NOT permanently sweet or cognitively empty.** His literal reading of language creates whole new argument paths; occasionally he notices hypocrisy or outmaneuvers a more confident adult. *Hands on a Hamburger* uses his misunderstanding of food rules and counting to make an apparently trivial contest socially and physically grotesque. *Rabbot* uses his insistence on playing against Shake's alleged leadership. He can be affectionate, malicious, vain, credulous, inventive, stubborn or accidentally insightful; let his decisions count.

**The dynamic:** Shake tries to own an experience he didn't create; Frylock argues science/correctness, sometimes intervening invasively; Meatwad's concrete literal interpretation uncovers the hypocrisy or starts a new chain, often to Shake's outrage. Their shared house and Carl's complaints mean they can neither have a stable family nor conclusively exile each other. None alone is the complete comic engine. Actual affection and resentment survive.

**BELLY adaptation, actual already-present cast:** The trio already shares protected BOYS CLUB with Chris, fictional Charles Manson and other legitimate residents; Billy is sovereign exterior/current owner, and K.K. voluntarily departed outside in E34. A working exterior Monitor 2, RDR2 Monitor 1, acid outside raised domestic platforms, CLEAR MEAT prison visibility/audibility, finite equipment and hoarded valuables, actual cooking/workbench facilities create unusually rich intersections. The interior itself is bizarre but residents increasingly treat it as a house. Exploit the physical irony: a dispute about breakfast can have a sealed-prison witness and a real digestive hazard without automatically hurting the exterior host or breaking barriers. Chris is a dangerous hoarding cook/marksman with his own personality, not appointed as a patient sitcom dad. Charles has independent manipulative perversity and preexisting responsibilities, not a passive jar thief. Billy can veto ingestion, entry or egress, misdirect through his banjo, or bargain in predatory old-fashioned ways; he is not a disembodied landlord or source of unlimited free props.

**Design constraint:** To be Runeterra's most dysfunctional household in STORY FEEL, make the shared space generate independent incompatible wants, ethical disagreements, inventive sabotage, avoidable consequences, occasional genuine affection and escalating insider grievances. "Most dysfunctional" is an authorial quality ambition, not automatic world-scale reputation.

**An optional, non-enacted causal ENGINE illustration:** a normal freezer dispute -> Shake falsely sells priority to food he doesn't own -> Meatwad honestly tries to enforce the invented category -> Frylock attempts a real supported monitoring workaround that accidentally proves Chris's inventory theft -> Charles claims the evidence supports warden authority -> Chris chooses the worst possible response -> Billy makes a separate decision. Do not prescribe this exact incident or invent parts/powers. No mandatory fixed chain length or replay of E33's old-chair sale.

## TPP: competing comic genres, one criminal body and a real threat

**Step Brothers / Prestige Worldwide:** adult men build a massive public identity with almost no underlying achievement; their immaturity, pride and sudden competence can collide at real life-scale. A presentation can collapse into genuine artistic talent or humiliating market failure. TPP should occasionally create a spectacle whose effort and failure are both consequential, NOT repeat real-estate or shipping forms every episode.

**Anchorman:** an ensemble owns a self-important brand that changes how outsiders treat it; nobody inside recognizes how absurd the team looks; childish territorial rivalry can become a deadly public incident, then a sincere moment. TPP can perceive petty status war as existential competition without all of Noxus sharing the joke.

**Dr Evil / Austin Powers:** Dr Evil's self-consciously theatrical tech, meetings, ransom and numbers are punctured by practical reality and a son's direct question; his incompetence does NOT preclude dangerous decisions. Austin, if later legitimately recruited, remains sincere, sometimes effective, fundamentally opposed to predatory abuse. Don't make Austin an obedient criminal just to meet a five-member enrollment list. Neither is physically present after E34.

**Spaceballs:** solemn authoritarian threat is wrecked by costumes, literal obstacles and self-aware presentation; sometimes the audience knows the fictional trope is being spoofed before the character does. The audience was intentionally misdirected by Clifford's E33 faux-Vader surgery; that reveal is already spent, not a recurring surprise. New fair shock-reveal farces need distinct and original set-ups.

**South Park Coon & Friends:** Cartman's superhero brand and personal status matter more to him than saving people. His naked self-interest can become actually violent betrayal while he insists he embodies justice. Original scripts show status politics, childish dependency and grand catastrophe in the same scene. Jimmy's actual comic timing and objections can be independent, not a canned stutter. Timmy is independent with constrained but real communication, action and willingness to refuse. They are children: do not sexualize or place them into adult sexual plots.

**Dutch:** an intelligent emotional opportunist from late-Chapter-3 RDR2, still grieving Bob; speaking charisma can recruit, threaten, lie or persuade. Don't flatten him into filler "I have a plan" every scene. His choices must cost loyalty or generate real opportunities and betrayals.

**Tracy Morgan:** the adult large conjoined host is ACTUAL fictionalized Tracy, not Busta; he is furious about the mistaken name and resents being bound to the miniature original ADULT HUMAN Brian. His independent refusal/escape ambitions, real ability to move the shared body and a grievance that doesn't disappear after one correction are vital. Not Family Guy's canine Brian.

**Clifford:** same adult Jack Marston, genuine E33 supported assisted walking, bent neck and unfinished tattoos, mobility limits and existing wheelchair; humiliating appearance does not erase revenge capacity. He is NOT actual Vader or his dead father John.

**Resource anchors after E34:** TPP netted fifty-nine new silver at Pell's office, retained the original cargo ledger, retains unresolved rent/medical balances, and earned an angry broker/witness risks. One adult hatchet attacker died by K.K., another suffered a shot wrist. Those are not generic recurring guard encounters, a magic war chest or a guarantee of immediate revenge.

**More faithful TPP mechanism:** Somebody launches an absurdly branded but real criminal operation; a different member exploits or undermines the presentation for personal advantage; outsiders react logically to fraud, threat or spectacle; the scheme either obtains genuine assets or provokes real damage/reputation/retaliation. Allow successful scheming, wickedness, betrayal between members, grotesque accidental fame and misrecognized celebrity conflict. Do not overexploit only contracts, tickets, rent, paperwork, badges or committee meetings as joke substitutions. TPP is a rival gang, not a harmless office parody.

## The comedy types the author chose, and the specific necessary difference

- **Transgressive surrealism:** a coherent social norm is challenged by bizarre/culturally taboo premises, and the people inside treat something hideous as ordinary.
- **Grotesque anti-comedy:** sincere buildup abruptly punctured by revolting banalism or disgusting concrete truth; then the result persists rather than magically resets.
- **Shock-reveal farce:** withhold one key identity/property FAIRLY, invest in the wrong but plausible audience inference, reveal a materially different truth with an irreverent crash. A surprise that cheats geography or invents a person is bad continuity, not a valid reveal.
- **Absurdist crossover satire:** exploit CONTRADICTORY full-franchise ethics, sizes, customs, speech and powers. A toy-sized Buzz trying to conduct a heroic rescue among gang criminals has a coherent mistaken frame; an unspecified "character cameo" does not.
- **Rick/Trover escalation:** initial absurd objective -> competing motives and rude interruptions -> specific bad decisions -> increasingly strange but actual changed circumstances. No mandatory canned three-beat choreography.

The earlier S3 Micah/Bob, Hawking/Colm/Brusta, Charles Fish/Manson transformations exemplify profound belief violation and grotesque aftermath; their existing manuscripts must be preserved and they must not be recreated as identical punchlines every episode.
