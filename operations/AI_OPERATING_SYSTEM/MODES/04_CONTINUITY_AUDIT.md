# MODE 04: CONTINUITY AUDIT
Activation: `AUDIT:`
Mode: WORK ONLY. No repair, no PLAY, no source mutation.

## Mission
Find contradictions, missing evidence, timeline errors, character-knowledge violations, mechanics violations, and state drift.

## Audit targets
- Current cursor, episode numbering, delivery and acceptance.
- Exact previous ending and checkpoint alignment.
- Chronology, elapsed time, geography, travel, and scene order.
- Character location, knowledge, agency, relationships, and ownership.
- Injuries, deaths, equipment, ammunition, abilities, damage, custody, and resources.
- Crossover/anomaly constraints and dimensional drag.
- Selected future beats incorrectly presented as occurred events.
- Duplicate, stale, conflicting, or incomplete records across platforms.

## Rules
- Read complete source owners and matching evidence, not only summaries.
- Compare source versions and scope. Last-edited time alone does not establish authority.
- Do not silently reconcile conflicts or repair manuscripts/ledgers.
- Classify findings as CONFIRMED, CONFLICT, MISSING, STALE, or INFERENCE.
- State "Data not established: [Topic]" where evidence is absent.
- Keep GitHub's authoritative state separate from Airtable/Notion projections. Google Drive is retired unless Kaze explicitly reactivates it.

## Output
Return a prioritized issue register with exact source paths, quoted/brief supporting evidence, impact, and the minimum evidence/action needed to resolve each issue. No writes unless separately authorized.


## Tool-access boundary
If a source platform is unavailable, identify the exact inaccessible source and classify it as PENDING. Audit only evidence actually retrieved or supplied. Never simulate cross-platform comparison, readback, or a successful check.
