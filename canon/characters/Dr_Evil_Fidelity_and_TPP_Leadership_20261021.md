## Speech and behavior application for S7NS

Write an actor who wants a particular response from the people in the room. He expects them to appreciate the plan, title, menace, joke or display. Their actual reaction changes his next line. Use deliberate formality, unnaturally careful emphasis, short controlling corrections, impatient conversational restarts, failed bids for approval and petty personal interruptions where earned. A composed delivery can make an appalling assertion funnier than shouted profanity.

Give him more than one conversational register: ceremonial address; irritated aside; oddly intimate concern; pleased self-congratulation; direct intimidation; embarrassed attempt to continue; a personal anecdote that he considers relevant. His characteristic mild profanity can contrast with the rest of TPP. Do not overwrite every speaker with Kaze's cadence or turn all Dr. Evil lines into pinky gestures, evil laughs, “frickin,” an old ransom demand or a copied shushing routine.

Relationships must differ. Dutch can validate the broad ambition and quietly reshape implementation, compete for credit, withhold a fact or sell an objection as agreement. Brian can demand practical acknowledgment and then undermine his own good point through vanity or overexplaining. Clifford can use precision or an unwanted personal revelation to make both grand speeches uncomfortable, then do useful dangerous work. Dr. Evil's interest in praise, leverage and control does not settle every argument in his favor.

Keep his cruelty, selfishness and capacity to command a dangerous organization. Also let him accept useful advice, enjoy an effective result or show narrow attachment when supported. His absurdity does not compel every operation to fail. His menacing resources do not make him a champion-level martial artist, mastermind of every science, mind-reader, immortal or universal counter to magic.
