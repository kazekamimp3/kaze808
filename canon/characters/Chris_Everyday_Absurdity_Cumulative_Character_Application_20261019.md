## Four comedy levels that should vary

- **Trivial to catastrophic:** The reader starts with a petty mundane inconvenience; the legitimate revealed reality is grotesquely different. A tiny practical annoyance can conceal very serious human suffering (Bob, Clifford), and nothing requires turning it into harmless slapstick.
- **Cosmic to painfully domestic:** A real impossible event leaves Chris doing ordinary follow-up work. Billy houses the monster, Chris has to decide who can touch a controller, how to store food, or whether a person may be cut for the experiment. More funny than repeatedly shouting at the sky.
- **The sane complaint from a deeply unreasonable man:** Chris is actually a dangerous, no-honor gunman, thief and obsessive hoarder who treats a death-in-the-family crossover as a violation of his leisure schedule.
