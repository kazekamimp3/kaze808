Closed.

His hips jerked forward.

Brian stopped.

A second Jawa clapped a rhythm on the cart.

The first leaned back, made an exaggerated effort noise and nearly fell off.

The adults around the cart howled.

Brian slowly lowered the bowl.

Behind them, one of the Tusken scouts performed an even larger version, elbows out, knees bent, every movement delivered with the grave commitment of a man demonstrating how to survive a sandstorm with his groin.

Closed.
