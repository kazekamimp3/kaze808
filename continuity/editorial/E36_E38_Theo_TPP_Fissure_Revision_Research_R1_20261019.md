The same metaphors repeatedly use routine office/administrative or generic disaster categories. The most original concept (each planet a distinct real bodily ordeal) gets flattened into a taxonomy; there are too few scene-dependent social detours, surprise category reversals, cruelly specific incidental details, false mini-endings or delayed callbacks. Children's refusals are commendably autonomous, but the gang's competing deranged self-images seldom *physically hijack* the plan.
