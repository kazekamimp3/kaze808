## The actual TPP comedy engine

**Steady escalating shared delusion.** Dutch announces a visionary plan; Brian invents titles, Tracy challenges the premise, Clifford is preoccupied with his humiliating revenge. Their attention shifts when they glimpse a *real* opportunity. Someone may execute the plan successfully while the others make it worse. The funny part isn't a yelled slogan: it's the unforeseen tangible consequences.

**Loyalty is brittle but meaningful.** They can abandon someone, rescue someone, lie to somebody they love, feel belittled or genuinely defend the group against the Sins, without becoming saints. A useful alliance may survive because of shared resentment rather than competence or complete trust.

**Stage rivalry with source-valid Anchorman bravado.** When rival crews cross, introductions, misnamed roles and overblown formations might occur, but actual geography, available weapons and casualties govern the outcome; one failed negotiation need not instantly become a full brawl. Their showboating can force a real escalation.

**Distinct voices.** Dutch's confidence expands through emotionally persuasive promises and repeated qualified belief; he can sincerely believe his own spin. Tracy is unpredictable yet emotionally legible, sometimes booming, sometimes brutally straightforward; he knows he isn't Busta. Brian's imaginary authority, DJ expertise and Dr Evil-inspired overengineering collide with real practical objections. Clifford is adult Jack Marston whose literacy, wounded pride, remembered parents and bitter sarcasm still exist; wheelchair dependence until the authored surgery is actually enacted.

**Not harmless idiots.** They steal, intimidate, retaliate, seriously damage rival criminal ventures, occasionally score meaningful wealth, and sometimes hurt people. Stupidity changes *how* they commit crimes, not whether real stakes matter. Not every episode needs TPP, but it must develop its own timelines and motives regularly enough to earn ongoing rivalry.

**No sitcom reset.** Rent obligations, unforgiven tattoo injuries, injured bodies, stolen cargo ledger, past Chris/Arthur humiliations, family grief and Brian's manipulative behavior carry forward. Comedy can puncture grave tension without erasing cost. Their own humiliation can create a new rivalry tactic rather than simply another silly insult.
