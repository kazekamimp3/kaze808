## High-potential ACTUAL narrative twists (ALL editorial brainstorm, not canon yet)
A. **Fortune as social weapon**: cookies are regular material printed with impossibly coincidental, intimate insults; no actual oracle, surveillance, cursed paper, hidden TPP infiltration or character-breaking narrator revelation is yet chosen. Each reacts differently: Arthur hears Dutch, Kaze hears a private truth, Dana jokes in baseline, K.K. seriously evaluates “axe” danger, Dana misreads Mana's body language. The insult triggers a real choice, not a queue of readers.
B. **Escalation trap**: the most innocent, competent restaurant people become the most bewildered by C/K's chosen villainy. The genre expects fodder NPCs; their prior competence and names make the slaughter tragic and dangerous, not a victimless show. All interior restaurant occupants die in selected revision; separate passersby outside may logically witness aftermath; no survivor inside is invented. Other Sins independently act/decline/argue, and cannot be forced into assent or an implausible moral freeze.
C. **Fissure has a second narrative job**: Kaze strikes locally with valid sword/terrain ability, and the resulting contiguous physical rupture causes a DIFFERENT later problem at the Immortal Bastion: broken aqueduct/underground infrastructure, Noxian defensive/tunnel access, a public political legitimacy crisis, credible retaliation. No instantly informed Swain or fresh Darkin crust power; careful local-to-capital geography.
D. **TPP inversions**: one preposterous public performance accidentally becomes objectively useful and grants the gang a real criminal opening; different members each want the credit. A status fight forces a material sacrifice or leaves an identifiable enemy. Risk and gain both rise, but all actions have real payment/gear/geography.
