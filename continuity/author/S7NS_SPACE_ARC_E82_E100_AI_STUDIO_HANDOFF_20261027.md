3. He then hears: “uncle, you get magic”. Dana opens his eyes. Venlee is somehow on Tatooine.
4. Dana: “Venlee? WHAT FUCK YOU DOING HERE.. ACTUALLY- WHY THE FUCK YOU WAKE ME UP!”
5. Venlee: “i asked chris to scrape his pipe, he said he mo more, and to ask you”

7. Dana turns green and runs faster and faster toward Venlee, wanting blood. Edward Kenway suddenly assassinates Venlee from the top of a building. Dana barely stops in time. As the dust clears, Dana has returned to baseline form and realizes someone took his kill.
8. Dana gets inches from Edward's face: “so you just gonna be a mahu and take my kill?” Edward: “a what?”
9. Dana remembers playing Black Flag: trying to parkour or chase a target, barely passing a beam or wall, only for Edward to glue to the thing, run halfway up and back down, and make Dana lose the mission.
10. Dana: “you fucken suck at parkour”. Before Edward can answer, Dana continues: “no, matter fact, you suck at swimming too! you even suck at being a fucken pirate. not fooling anybody with those white teeth. i've seen white dog shit look swim better than you-”

12. Chris watches with the complete satisfaction of an unbearable bunghole itch finally scratched by a calculated P.S.I. fart at tremendous velocity. Chris turns to Kaze: “tremendous.. i farted”.
