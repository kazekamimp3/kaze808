ACT I: TAMPON PANDEMIC PRODUCTIONS & THE DOUBLE-HOMICIDE (RESTART BEAT 1)

  - Beat 1: The Chronic Humiliation & The Scotch-Taped Empire
      - Setting: T.P.P. (Tampon Pandemic Productions) inner office—a
        fluorescent-lit, grease-stained shithole smelling of stale vape juice
        and unwashed taint.
      - Dynamics: Brian (2'2", small-man syndrome, recently scrubbed of orange
        paint and glued pubic hair) attempts to pitch himself as a terrifying,
        high-tier criminal mastermind to an audience that views him as a
        pathetic joke. Tracy Morgan (mistaken by the gang for Busta Rhymes)
        glares from the corner with mini-Brian on his shoulder. Clifford (adult
        Jack Marston) rambles off-meds about his dead dog, and Master Shake
        drinks through a dirty straw.
      - Dutch’s Hypocritical Seed: Brian rants about getting bloody revenge
        against the Seven Sins. Dutch van der Linde, cleaning a dirty revolver,
        dismisses him with cold, pseudo-philosophical arrogance: "Revenge is a
        fool’s game, son."
  - Beat 2: The School-Shooter Snap (The Double-Homicide)
      - The Setup: Brian snaps under the cumulative weight of years of
        disrespect. Having swiped Clifford’s unused red lightsaber from a
        laundry pile earlier, he pulls it out, hoping for respect. Clifford and
        Shake laugh directly in his face.
      - The Carnage: Brian ignites the blade and bifurcates Clifford at the
        waist. The unshielded plasma flash-boils Clifford’s lower organs,
        causing a steaming, high-pressure explosion of liquefied chili-mac,
        bowels, and shredded denim across the wallpaper.
      - Master Shake Liquefied: Master Shake slips in Clifford's steaming pelvic
        sludge, shrieking like a tea kettle. Brian stomps on Shake's face with a
        boot and hacks him repeatedly into a smoking, chunky puddle of
        strawberry-flavored marinara paste and shattered plastic.
  - Beat 3: The Frantic Escape & Dutch’s Vendetta Engine
      - The Panic: Brian stands soaked in boiling human and synthetic viscera,
        hyperventilating in sheer psychological terror at his own butchery. He
        flees out the back door into the Noxian badlands.
      - Dutch’s Shattered Philosophy: Dutch enters, stepping ankle-deep into
        Master Shake’s warm marinara soup. Seeing Clifford bisected on the
        floor, Dutch completely abandons his "fool's game" philosophy, breaking
        down into hysterical grief. He runs to Dr. Evil, begging for an all-out,
        blood-feud syndicate hunt to slaughter Brian. Dr. Evil responds with
        cold, manipulative, self-absorbed detachment.

      - The Performance: Brian shoves his crinkled rap journal into Starkiller's

      - The Signature: Starkiller stares with deadpan, homicidal exhaustion,

        Force-push.
      - The Trajectory: Brian screams across the stratosphere like a biological
        missile, rocketing through atmospheric layers outbound toward Tatooine.

ACT III: TATOOINE CRASH, CELLBLOCK MISERY & TARMAC CARNAGE (RESTART BEAT 3 / E82)

  - Beat 7: The Jawa Crater & The Unconscious Cult
      - The Impact: Brian craters into the Dune Sea at terminal velocity,
        landing square on an enslaved Jawa. The kinetic force obliterates the
        Jawa's iron shackles, allowing the terrified creature to sprint away,
        while Brian is knocked stone-cold unconscious.
      - The Sleepwalking Routine: While blacked out, Brian's body kicks into his
        spastic sleepwalking loop: violent, rhythmic hip-thrusting, heavy
        grunting, and flapping his legs open to moon the horizon.
      - The Cult Forms: The surrounding enslaved Jawas and Tusken Raiders
        mistake this spastic rhythm for an ancient liberation dance and begin
        enthusiastically imitating his hip-thrusts in a massive, synchronized
        desert ritual.
  - Beat 8: The Dungeon Reveal (Deadpool Chained)
      - The Framing Payoff: Hard cut to Jabba's subterranean dungeon. Deadpool
        (Wade) is chained to a slimy limestone wall, turning to the audience to
        reveal he’s been narrating this entire saga while trapped next to Brian.
      - The Physical Rule: Brian is awake now—meaning the hip-thrusting stops
        completely—and he relentlessly assaults Wade with rapid-fire, unhinged
        comic-book trivia ("Could Wolverine's healing factor survive a cow-bee
        sting to the nuts?").
      - Jabba’s Shakedown: Jabba ("Uncle Coco" Joey Diaz speech fidelity)
        waddles into the dungeon corridor. He shakes them down for 22,000
        Brapples in property damage for the broken Jawa shackle, calls Brian a
        "defective sex toy," and rambles about running scams in North Bergen
        back in '84 before locking them down.
  - Beat 9: Cockpit Chaos & The Cosmic Roadkill (The Sins Inbound)
      - The Driving Lesson: In the cockpit of the newly renamed Aluminum Falcon,
        Kaze and Chris argue at the top of their lungs over who gets to fly.
        Kaze claims his Grand Theft Auto experience makes him qualified. Dana
        backs Kaze up, while Arthur huddles in the corner, terrified of space
        travel.
      - The Sphinx Smash: Kaze takes the yoke, sends the ship into a violent
        death spiral, and demolishes the alternate-timeline Sphinx on Tatooine
        before tearing ass out of orbit. Jabba, Wade, and Brian watch the crash
        from the sand, with Jabba calling Han Solo "half a fag" and launching
        into a rambling mafia story.
      - The Hawking T-Bone & Galactus Execution: Out in deep space, Kaze
        accidentally T-bones alternate-timeline Stephen Hawking's automated
        chair (leaving Han Solo to take the 4.8B Brapple blame). Kaze claims he
        needs to use the bathroom, steps out into the vacuum of space holding
        his breath, and punches clean through Galactus's skull like a human
        cannonball. Mountains of neon-purple celestial brain matter detonate
        into the void, obliterating a nearby planet, while Kaze walks back into
        the Falcon cockpit covered in gore. Chris screams: "CAN WE AT LEAST MAKE
        IT TO TATOOINE BEFORE YOU DO ALL THAT HOODRAT NIGGA SHIT?!"
  - Beat 10: Tarmac Arrival & The "You Get Magic?" Massacre
      - The Skid: Chris crash-lands the Aluminum Falcon on the Tatooine tarmac.
        Dana, completely drained by Kaze's space atrocities, passes out in the
        cockpit under the rule that nobody wakes him up.
      - The Wake-Up: Venlee appears on Tatooine scraping his pipe, whispering
        "You get magic?" into Dana's ear. Dana wakes up in a murderous
        gamma-rage, transforms into his 8'10", 840 lb green brute form, and
        sprints after Venlee.
      - The Kill-Steal & The Roast: Edward Kenway drops from a rooftop and
        assassinates Venlee, stealing Dana's kill. Dana gets nose-to-nose with
        Edward, brutally roasting his terrible parkour, swimming, and fake white
        teeth.
      - Arm Obliteration & The Sack: Edward swings; Dana catches the arm,
        squeezes it until the bones burst out through the skin, and screams:
        "HAHA FAKE PIRATE, DANA MAKE FAKE PIRATE DROWN!" Dana stuffs Edward into
        a potato sack and slams him into the ground until his skull splatters
        like a wet watermelon.
      - Looting & Flatulence: Chris compulsively strips Edward's corpse
        completely butt-ass naked on the tarmac, equipping the assassin robes
        and hidden blades while screaming about Venlee deleting his 95% Black
        Flag save file. Chris watches Dana finish the kill and turns to Kaze:
        "tremendous.. i farted."
  - Beat 11: The Bounty Hunter Standoff & Tactical Disengagement
      - The Convergence: Boba Fett and Cad Bane pull up on the tarmac, drawn by
        Han Solo's 4.8B bounty and the smoke. They find a naked corpse, an 8'10"
        gamma monster, a 1,700 lb catfish with a banjo (Billy), a 1,400 lb pit
        bull (Mana), and Kaze with dried Galactus blood under his fingernails.
      - The Draw: Cad Bane starts a slow, menacing Duros drawl. Kaze cuts him
        off: "CAN YOU GET TO THE FUCKEN POINT PLEASE?" Cad Bane instantly draws
        and shoots Kaze point-blank in the chest.
      - The Healing & The Exit: Kaze's BLACKLIGHT/Darkin flesh instantly knits
        itself back together while he stares blankly. Arthur Morgan steps
        forward, thumbing his revolvers: "I'd reckon you think twice 'bout
        pulling that trigger again, partner."
      - The Recalculation: Following strict, realistic professional fidelity,
        Boba Fett and Cad Bane recognize an impossible biological hazard with
        zero ROI. They don't panic; they execute a cold, tactical withdrawal to
        track the bounties through smarter channels.

ACT IV: UNDERWORLD LOGISTICS, SPICE ADDICTION & THE RANCOR PIT (E83–E87)

  - Beat 12: Falcon Repairs & Scrapyard Scraping (E83–E84)
      - The FRP Drain: The Falcon is critically damaged (cherry-gel
        contamination from Kool-Aid Man, breached hull, shot cooling lines).
        Chris is forced to navigate strict Falcon Reserve Points (FRP), unable
        to jump to hyperspace.
      - Billy in the Basin: Local Jawas attempt to dismantle Catfish Billy while
        he floats in a moisture-evaporator basin wearing his Giganotosaurus hat.
        Billy swallows a salvage droid whole, casually digesting it while
        conversing through his internal throat-valves.
  - Beat 13: Batuna/Marcelina & The Cow-Bee High (E85)
      - Unified Identity: Batuna and Marcelina are confirmed as one single 4-ton
        Hutt entity—Jabba’s girlfriend, who secretly commands her own luxury
        star-yacht, security grid, and spice syndicate.
      - The Infatuation: Upstairs in Jabba’s palace, Bee Moo (having completed
        his radioactive cow-bee transformation) is supplied his first hit of
        pure Spice directly by Batuna/Marcelina. The two engage in a greasy,
        drug-addled romance behind Jabba’s back, cementing Bee Moo’s permanent,
        non-resettable addiction storyline.
  - Beat 14: The Pyke Spice Hijack & Kaze’s 100M Bounty (E85)
      - The Contract: Jabba hires the Sins to hijack an armored Pyke Syndicate
        spice convoy.
      - The Bloodbath: Kaze turns the heist into a chaotic massacre using Black
        Marrow and Black Fire, annihilating the convoy. The heist succeeds, but
        Kaze earns an independent 100-million-Brapple bounty on his own head,
        separate from Han’s debt.
  - Beat 15: The Double-Cross & The Throne Slicing (E86)
      - The Trap: Jabba invites the Sins to his court to settle their cut, only
        to hit the floor lever and drop them into the Rancor Pit.
      - The Meat Processing: Catfish Billy uses full BELLY mechanics to
        physically swallow the Rancor whole, groaning with heavy reptilian
        indigestion.
      - The Slicing: Kaze doesn't use the stairs; he channels spatial-rupture
        energy through Black Marrow and vertically cleaves Jabba’s throne room
        floor from beneath, slicing the massive stone dais in half. Kaze hoists
        Jabba upside down by his blubbery tail over the chasm, forcing an
        immediate, terrified renegotiation of terms in pure Joey Diaz panic.

ACT V: THE LUXURY YACHT HEIST, THE STAR-FORGE & THE NOXIAN HORIZON (E88–E100)

  - Beat 16: Nar Shaddaa & Infiltrating the Velvet Star (E88–E89)
      - The Gala: The Sins track Batuna/Marcelina to Nar Shaddaa, infiltrating a
        high-society underworld gala aboard her luxury star-yacht, the Velvet
        Star.
      - The Smuggling: Chris smuggles heavy firearms past biological scanners
        using Billy’s BELLY storage. Dana struggles in ill-fitting formal wear,
        while Arthur stakes out the administrative vault.
  - Beat 17: The Bearer-Warrant Cipher Ledger (E90–E91)
      - The Vault Breach: Arthur uses his crimson cyber-eye to bypass the
        physical security of the central vault, securing the physical Cipher
        Ledger (requiring its 4 strict authentication layers: physical original,
        live biometric match, paired hardware key, and signing core).
      - The Hostage Counterplay: Kaze takes Batuna/Marcelina hostage for ransom.
        Proving she is not a passive victim, Marcelina triggers an internal
        ship-wide lockdown with her private security, turning the yacht into a
        compartmentalized death maze and trapping the Sins inside her
        administrative web.
  - Beat 18: Hondo’s Deal & The Double-Agent Exposure (E92–E95)
      - The 5% Escape: Hondo Ohnaka arrives offering an orbital extraction
        for 50% of the score. Kaze threatens to feed Hondo’s entire pirate crew
        to Billy, negotiating him down to 5%. Hondo leaves with a useless
        metadata-only copy of the ledger.
      - The Siege & Reveal: During the defense of the Forgotten Orbital Station,
        Arthur reveals he intercepted Hondo’s secret Black Sun transmission two
        days prior. Arthur springs a brutal counter-ambush, cutting off Hondo’s
        escape routes with cold frontier efficiency.
  - Beat 19: The Star-Forge Assault & The Dimensional Bridge (E96–E99)
      - The Cosmic Weapon: Decrypting the physical ledger reveals the
        coordinates to the ancient Star-Forge, which has been secretly automated
        as a cosmic livestock engine feeding on galactic misery.
      - Reactor Warfare: The Sins launch a full-scale assault on the reactor
        core. Dana uses automated gun turrets like baseball bats to smash
        through cybernetic clone legions; Chris executes ruthless gun-fu with
        The Critic; Billy swallows the station’s physical control core.
      - The Dimensional Tear: Kaze redirects the massive output of the
        Star-Forge core through Black Marrow, violently ripping open a
        permanent, screaming dimensional bridge connecting the deep cosmos
        directly to Runeterra.
  - Beat 20: Landfall & The Horizon (E100)
      - The Aftermath: The smoke clears. The dimensional bridge stabilizes into
        a permanent transit scar across the sky. The surviving crew pulls
        through the wreckage onto the red soil of Noxus. Damaged equipment,
        scars, and lingering debts are logged.
      - The Quiet Landing: Zero speeches. Zero grand cosmic fanfare. Kaze sits
        down on an overturned wooden shipping crate, pops the tab on an ice-cold
        can of Pepsi, takes a slow sip, and stares quietly out toward the
        untamed Noxian horizon.
