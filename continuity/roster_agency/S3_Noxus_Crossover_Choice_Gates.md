Courtly questions can conceal ruthless calculations, and his demon-sourced reports are incomplete. A credible marine/port report can make him compare testimony and choose a bounded response. An incorrect decision may genuinely cost him. He doesn't know every crossover mind or watch all ravens. Owner: `character_sources/season3/Swain_Character_Source.txt`.

A disrupted supply route might make him choose troops, evacuation, patrol discipline or refusal rather than an arbitrary duel. Do not impose grief for the erased old-branch Draven death. Owner: `character_sources/season3/Darius_Character_Source.txt`.

**Draven.** Craves the public spectacle that sustains his standing, but remains technically lethal. A prestigious outsider could be publicity, a rival, or an unwelcome diversion. Audience and venue must actually be present; he doesn't fight every import on schedule. Darius relationship remains active. Owner: `character_sources/season3/Draven_Character_Source.txt`.

**LeBlanc.** Works to preserve the Black Rose's centuries of leverage, against Swain and other genuine threats. ## Every choice must pass the same evidence boundary

A person's *motivation* is not a guarantee they win, arrive, ally, betray, seduce, die or fight. First locate them at their source era, keep their kit and own history intact, establish what they know, what they want today and why another person is in the way, then let them choose and live with consequences. Family, debt, fraud, coercion, professional pride, spiritual duty, trauma, desire and boredom should not collapse into the same universal motivation.

**Extended/regional actors:** the individually scoped 112-entry roster and 70-entry women/age/region map are preserved in the project's S7NS Library; this guide is not a replacement for their complete source owners. Especially preserve Kayn/Rhaast contested single-body mechanics, darkin weapon-host gates, dead ancient Darkin and discontinued ballista, independent TPP rivalry and the author-directed loyal return of Dana and Mana. No automatic woman's hookup or regional teleport.
