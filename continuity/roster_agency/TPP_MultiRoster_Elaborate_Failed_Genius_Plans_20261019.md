Claudia? No: use actual **Clifford**. No separate John Marston or actual Vader.

## Proposed elaborate anti-Sins plots: optional starting points, not scheduled completions

### The Grand Serpent Maritime Safety Monopoly
**Want:** Dutch needs legitimate-looking shipping fees and rent money; Zid wants to make the Sins look like reckless invaders; Cartman wants licensed superhero credit; Dr. Evil wants an enormous ceremonial headquarters.
**Actual seed:** E32 real cargo ledger with redirected shipments plus the actual World Serpent's body and damaged harbor. Requires an actual port/customs contact, guards' knowledge and paperwork, and physical route—not magical new authority.
**Idiot mechanism:** Rename a dockside salvage tent the Official Anti-Snake Maritime Authority; sell fake safe-passage passes, absurdly specific body-part liability waivers and certificates stamped with a giant drawing of the serpent's anus. Brian demands a licensing fee for the logo; Tracy correctly objects to signing as Busta and refuses to take the ticket booth; Dutch insists the fee structure is a visionary freedom movement. Zid proposes positioning signs where the Sins might arrive.
**Collision:** A real Noxian warmason, naval pilot, corrupt official, Bilgewater captain or stranded merchant may refuse, expose, buy or take over the fraudulent service. The serpent is intelligent and may move independently, making the "safe" route dangerous.
**Consequences:** Some fees may be collected, actual victims may demand refunds, Noxians investigate, or a better equipped pirate steals the ledger. Nothing is guaranteed by outline.

### Project Fake Sins Family Reunion
**Want:** Zid seeks the sweet humiliation of making his old friends look gullible. Clifford wants Arthur embarrassed. Brian wants TPP's name on the invitation. Dutch wants a buyer's money and a distracting spectacle.
**Means:** Only after Zid is actually accepted, access to a recently established **public** Sin reputation detail and an actually reachable venue, not an invented gang base or secret BELLY map.
**Idiot mechanism:** A giant counterfeit banquet/family reunion, paid actors claiming to be gang associates, forged “loyalty certificates,” a criminal auction and a statue of Kaze sculpted from cheap confiscated candle wax. Zid insists the roast is surgical psychological warfare, yet cannot explain who is supposed to attend or why. Tracy refuses to be the musical headliner. Dr. Evil wants to charge admission to the execution that hasn't happened. Jimmy may be the only person checking the logistics.
**Collision:** Real witnesses or any available Sins may ignore it, exploit the idiots' stage, expose counterfeits or turn the attempted humiliation against rivals. Other crossover visitors can pursue the fake auction for their OWN goals, not because the list demands a cameo.
**Cost:** Fake tickets, costume labor, rented space, ruined reputations, real insult debts. Hilarious scheme may even win money before collapsing.

### Operation Grandfather's Revenge: the Walking Clifford Launch
**Want:** Clifford wants to walk again and make the Sins afraid; Dutch wants the dramatic comeback of a mythic gunslinger; Dr. Evil wants a revenue stream from villainy; Zid wants to help for approval.
**Means:** Author-approved future shoddy Vader-like surgery must actually be staged, tools/doctor/repair parts/physically credible mobility mechanisms established, and the patient revealed as Clifford, not Anakin. No free spinal regrowth.
**Idiot mechanism:** Market Clifford's reconstruction as "Darth Clifford: The Return of the Refund"; steal a projected silhouette, dramatic breathing machine and cheap black metal mask. Tracy shouts that nobody hired him to play Busta at the launch. Brian declares himself executive Sith producer. The Coon claims the theatrical vigilante rights.
**Collision:** Arthur and Chris remember Clifford's actual history, though not future scenes. A Noxian surgeon, Starkiller, Maul, Austin Powers or real war veterans might react independently if genuinely present. The fake costume cannot grant actual Star Wars powers. A credible first use of his new legs can be genuinely dangerous even if the helmet makes a fart noise.
**Cost:** Recovery, pain, funding, power supply, and resentment; no automatic victory or forced rematch.

### The World's Worst Counter-Bounty
**Want:** Zid wants revenge/recognition, Brian wants corporate structure, Dutch needs payment, Dr. Evil enjoys elaborate formal threats. Cartman wants an embossed bounty on people who insulted him.
**Means:** Real witnessed crimes, legitimate/illegitimate buyers who actually reach TPP, and knowledge particular targets are alive in reachable areas.
**Idiot mechanism:** Sell contradictory overlapping bounties on Sins' masks, knees, furniture, liquor bottles and hypothetical “classified hair samples”; hire mercenaries through a complicated fake reverse auction whose contract language forces the winning bidder to pay TPP instead. Dr. Evil explains the auction in a forty-slide presentation; Tracy walks out as far as their shared body allows, taking Brian with him.
**Collision:** Samira may reject an insulting contract; Deadpool is narrator-only from E33 and **cannot physically accept or mock** a TPP offer; another genuinely reachable contractor must respond independently; Darius can detain fraudsters; Bilgewater pirates may decide TPP is easier to rob than the Sins. Buyers keep agency and valid combat strengths.
**Cost:** Reputation, money, real retaliation and possible permanent injuries. An elaborate plan does not auto-nerf Sins or surrender allies.

## High energy without boring repetition
A crazy plan should escalate through **three different person's bad decisions**, expose at least one genuinely competent counter-move, and end on a change (payment, object custody, injury, audience reputation, lost trust) where appropriate. No fixed three-beat quota: the rule is causal richness. Tracy's furious refusal can derail profitable plans; Brian can know one obscure technical thing; Jimmy can be right; Dutch can deliver a persuasive speech; children can leave or object; Zid can be laughably stupid and *still* hurt the Sins. Don't turn him into an unstoppable genius or force TPP to lose every encounter. Preserve actual physical and money continuity.
