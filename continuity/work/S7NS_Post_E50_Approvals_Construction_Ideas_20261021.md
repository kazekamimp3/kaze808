These are new assistant PROPOSALS, not included in the author's approval of earlier lists and not occurred scenes.

1. Kaze's uniquely precise Chris bait: he knows which ordinary question, badly timed helpful gesture, withheld salvage detail or silence Chris cannot leave alone. Chris tries to continue the real task and voluntarily reopens the argument; Kaze barely needs to contribute. Sometimes Chris catches it early and turns the setup back on him. Preserve chef competence and no false-skim-paranoia.
2. A quiet preparation scene before the solo inquiry: Arthur physically hands over Maul's actual hilt; each friend responds differently to Kaze's decision. Practical route/return questions and a badly timed Chris joke convey attachment without compulsory moral speeches. The group continues its own existing business during his actual travel.
3. The saber inquiry lets two damaged people disagree about allegiance and responsibility. Showing a hilt establishes neither ownership knowledge nor guilt. Starkiller may reject Kaze's question, answer narrowly, offer terms or ask his own questions. His real past doesn't automatically make him Maul's associate. Leave outcome open; actual conduct earns the branch.
4. Let the no-loose-ends stance create internal friction over evidence, timing or acceptable exposure. A disagreement need not make another Sin a pacifist or Kaze infallible. Someone can share ruthlessness and still contest a specific choice. Preserve the author's intended released-enemy target, not a new threat to murder a Sin.
5. Treat animals as consequential parts of the world: Mahnah chooses a real hunt, an owner's missing mount changes access, an enemy reacts to deliberate cruelty. Predation can intrude into an ordinary scene without being turned into a cute offscreen snack. Do not make an animal-death quota or a cruelty demonstration for every arrival.
6. Separate Shake's and Meatwad's departure motives. Shake could refuse the household's blame for the death he caused; Meatwad could leave because the life he chose there has changed. They decide rather than receive exile, and need not form a new permanent traveling duo. Charles tries to manage the departure's story/interview while the others want to get on with leaving. Actual camera and belongings remain with whoever legitimately takes/keeps them.
7. Billy continues his anecdote while fighting for an objective, offended that his opponent is not listening. The opponent attacks or interrupts anyway; a dodge/action becomes a conversational interruption rather than frozen combat. Use supported history or explicit boasting. Hearing Billy's ridiculous familiarity with danger can be formidable without forcing the listener to respect him.
8. Make restored mobility change Clifford's presentation: he crosses the room to answer an insult or chooses to remain standing through a speech. Arthur andChris now have a mobile antagonist to bully; removal of the chair does not reconcile them or erase his neck/tattoos. Avoid introducing replacement disability or new powers.
9. Starkiller's TPP/Clifford intersection remains selected development, but need not crowd Kaze's first solo meeting. A later causally available intersection can develop that direction after the inquiry has actually changed something.

No blocking question is required. Working interpretations: Vincent/Kaiser refer to Kaze; unspecified rest-of-gang animal attitudes retain individual sources; literal raw note retained in author owner.
