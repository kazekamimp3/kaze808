## What needs correcting

The recent character comedy is working. The larger season needs more follow-through: old intentions should turn into decisions, collisions and endings. More simultaneous subplots would make that harder.

1. **Some current source fields are stale.** The outline still describes Starkiller as prospective, a future Maul bargain, Tracy/Brian as conjoined and Clifford's surgery as unperformed. Later overrides contain the right history, but contradictory current-looking fields invite mistakes. Repair those fields rather than making the author babysit contradictions.
2. **Brian/Dr. Evil have become the richest recurring pair.** Keep that strength. Clifford needs his articulate, unsolicited history and genuine grievances back; Billy needs substantial unasked glory-day stories and choices. They should sometimes take over a scene instead of supplying the last reaction.
3. **The narrator can sound too neutral.** Deadpool is currently narrator-only by explicit author instruction. His weak presence is a voice problem we can repair now. A physical comeback would change that rule and remains a separate proposal.
4. **Too much practical detail reaches the listener.** Resource continuity matters, but repeated accounting, route qualifications and explanation of why something cannot happen slow the episode. Keep exact bookkeeping in the checkpoint; dramatize the money when it changes a choice.
5. **Humiliation needs variety.** Everyone can shit on Brian without everyone having the same joke. Authority, size, founding credit, taste, vanity and hypocrisy give different people different ammunition. Occasionally let him be right, then make his demand for recognition wreck the win.
6. **Danger needs follow-through too.** A formidable arrival loses force if the character only promises future action. Let violence or a consequential refusal actually settle something when the circumstances support it.
7. **Grief and wrongdoing need residue.** Daniel's absence, Kaze's loss of K.K., Tracy's death and Clifford's resentment should affect mundane decisions occasionally. A death cannot be only a ledger entry followed by a reset.

Politics: roughly halve the recent explanatory passages. A strong Bastion scene can show one pressure, a disagreement, a decision and its consequence. Swain and Darius can remain intelligent while spending fewer words explaining intelligence. Show the order changing life elsewhere instead of repeating the briefing from another angle.

## K.K. and the planting

E46 was written as a real death: Maul destroyed the essential heartwood and regeneration stopped. The burial near K.K.'s independently planted ordinary pear tree did not establish a surviving seed. The planting imagery made your reading understandable, but it was not secretly established survival.

Your new instruction selects the regrowth mechanic going forward. Groot-derived regrowth has official precedent: Disney/Marvel's I Am Groot production brief describes new growth from the original Groot's splinters. This supports the inspiration, not a claim that every version has identical identity rules or that the original E46 furnace death already left a living core.
Reference: https://lumiere-a.akamaihd.net/v1/documents/i_am_groot_production_brief_final_7-26-22_daf153b8.pdf (page2).

**Recommended construction, still proposed:** growth begins slowly from the actual buried material. Weather, water, nutrients and elapsed time matter. First show a small sign, then leave it alone for a while. A later return retains K.K.'s unnerving humor and agency; I favor continuity with the K.K. they knew over an automatically cute replacement child. Exact memory, identity, growth conditions and limits need deliberate construction. Avoid instant full size, endless spare clones or extending resurrection to Daniel and other dead characters. K.K. can remain an independent recurring character rather than adding another permanent passenger.

## Plot ideas that add depth without crowding episodes

Use two substantial foreground threads as a working preference, with an occasional short third beat only when it changes something. This is a flexible editorial recommendation, not a new quota. Characters can intersect briefly, make a consequential choice and leave the foreground.

1. **Starkiller changes TPP's transport problem.** His actual last direction is toward the coast for transport/information. If reachable routes bring them together, TPP sees an intimidating asset; he sees people offering terms. Their attempt to command or package him exposes his intolerance for another master. His choice can help one immediate operation and ruin TPP's intended control. No permanent recruitment or replayed entrance.
2. **Starkiller meets Clifford's imitation Vader.** Clifford thinks the suit earns professional recognition. Starkiller has reasons to find the resemblance offensive. Clifford's actual suffering can complicate that first contempt without creating instant friendship, Force abilities or mandatory redemption.
3. **Shang Tsung supplies one real useful service.** He earns trust through something his pinned MK1 version can actually do, then tries to trade upward through a lie or betrayal. Tie it to an existing need; do not import the Well or make him a universal parasite surgeon. A short consequential arc can honor the endorsed fatal-exit direction instead of expanding the permanent cast.
4. **Mahnuh's thefts create the Sins/TPP collision.** Merchants already have a reason to want a solution. TPP can exploit that demand while Chris and Billy protect their own complicity and Daynuh catches recognizable behavior. Begin from the thefts already enacted in41; the poker behavior from50 adds a new learned problem rather than replacing the old one.
5. **One operation, incompatible definitions of success.** Sins want access, TPP wants credit/payment, an outsider wants passage. A temporary overlap creates friction without three separate quests. The outcome should change who gets the thing they came for.
6. **Daniel's killer becomes knowable through evidence.** A reachable witness, credible description or Tai Lung's own disclosure can eventually turn grief into disagreement over what to do. No instant deduction or mandatory revenge march. The emotional conflict belongs to existing people.
7. **Finish Frylock's selected exit and let the household change.** Shake tries to control the story afterward. Meatwad and Charles respond differently; Billy has his own judgment. An empty place or a task nobody can now do carries the consequence without a new mourning subplot every episode.
8. **Riddick gets a bounded intersection.** His pursuit of freedom intersects an existing escape or transport route; cooperation has an end point. If his endorsed fatal arc is developed, earn the fatal circumstances using his actual competence. Do not make him a long-term bodyguard first.
9. **Political decisions become physical inconvenience or opportunity.** A decision about the actual fissure or access changes one route, supply or encounter the foreground characters experience. Short Bastion cause, visible local consequence. No assumption that every nuisance is a master plan.
10. **K.K.'s growth occupies occasional space between arcs.** One quiet, unsettling sign can sustain anticipation. His eventual return should alter a relationship and force a choice; avoid an immediate additional rescue expedition and full-time recruitment.

These are alternatives and possible combinations, not ten promised consecutive episodes. Avoid connecting every mystery to the Black Rose or arranging a council of every recognizable crossover.

## Dialing up the humor

- **Absurd status:** Dr. Evil delivers a grave address while Brian keeps relocating an ordinary object so he can see. Each correction makes the room's staging worse. The physical goal stays clear; no identical chair-climbing replay.
- **Grotesque intimacy:** Billy tells a revolting prehistoric memory with the sincerity of a treasured family anecdote. Someone is genuinely interested, making Chris more furious than disgust alone would.
- **Dark consequence:** Shake tries to minimize his responsibility with an excuse so selfish it damages his case. Let another character's silence land before the next joke.
- **Social horror:** K.K., if returned, offers a considerate observation whose underlying image is deeply wrong. Preserve deliberate wit; do not reduce him to random nonsense or a gentle mascot.
- **Misplaced admiration:** Brian can turn a grim logistical discussion into an increasingly indefensible Busta comparison. Someone else catches the pattern and refuses to give him the expected opening; Brian works harder.
- **Occasional anti-payoff:** Brian braces for the short joke, but Arthur attacks the quality of his reasoning. Brian is offended that the preparation was wasted.
- **Sleeping escalation:** The exact Chris shirt-lasso/turn/bright hairy backside/kick/wake-and-deny anecdote remains selected and unperformed. Give it a real shared night and a first-time witness when appropriate; do not make it obligatory every bedtime.

Escalation comes from what a person risks, reveals or ruins. More profanity alone will not make the scene darker or funnier. Leave room for genuine menace, affection and uncomfortable quiet.

## Relationship behavior menu

All below are proposed behaviors, not universal traits imposed on every scene. For actors not presently together, real travel, information and access must precede an encounter.

### Sins and their household

- **Kaze / Chris:** Switch who is the expert. Chris teaches a practical craft and overhelps; Kaze teaches gambling and realizes Chris has spread the lesson irresponsibly. Each can use a previous favor as ammunition.
- **Chris / Daynuh:** Let them argue through shared history and specific habits. Chris knows exactly what Daynuh will notice about Mahnuh and conceals the wrong detail. Daynuh can win an exchange through recognition rather than another lecture.
- **Daynuh / Mahnuh:** Familiar routines, different responses to tone, food-driven wandering and learned cues. The dog returns innocently; Daynuh recognizes the evidence on him. Keep Mahnuh nonverbal, canine and without Ki.
- **Kaze / Arthur:** Arthur can challenge a risky choice privately without becoming a moral supervisor. Kaze sometimes acts on the criticism later without announcing that Arthur was right.
- **Arthur / Chris:** Quiet practical cooperation followed by precise mutual insults. Competence gives their affection weight; every exchange need not become chaos.
- **Arthur / Daynuh:** Different patience with the same irresponsible friend. One starts defending Chris, hears the complete story and changes his mind mid-sentence.
- **Billy / Chris:** Competition over whose memory is more impressive; actual long anecdotes, corrections and interruptions. Sometimes Billy's apparently irrelevant story pays off in useful knowledge. Let Billy want the floor.
- **Billy / Kaze:** Mutual familiarity with danger and unequal ideas about what counts as a normal problem. Billy can independently disagree or decline to support an idiotic plan.
- **Charles / Meatwad:** Charles manufactures hierarchy; Meatwad cooperates for companionship, then casually makes the decision everyone follows. Keep moments when Charles sincerely enjoys his company.
- **Charles / Shake:** Rival claims to authority, each certain the other is the household's embarrassment. A temporary alliance collapses over whose name gets attached to it.
- **Meatwad / Shake / Frylock:** Affection, dependence, scientific pride and exploitation should coexist before the selected death. Afterward nobody inherits Frylock's competence automatically.
- **Household / visiting Sins:** Different hosts, food boundaries, private possessions and interview preferences. Their lives inside BELLY should continue when the exterior plot is serious; no magical observation or unauthorized exit.

### TPP and cross-faction friction

- **Brian / Dr. Evil:** Brian copies posture and pinky behavior before admitting admiration. Dr. Evil notices and adjusts his own gesture just to make Brian wrong. A useful correction earns a grudging acknowledgment Brian ruins by demanding public credit. Acceptance as Mini-Me remains Dr. Evil's choice; Brian keeps his identity.
- **Dutch / Brian:** Dutch praises Brian's contribution when recruiting his effort, then describes the result as his own vision. Brian remembers the exact wording and chooses the worst public moment to object.
- **Clifford / Brian:** Brian treats him like hired spectacle; Clifford demands respect for actual survival and capability. Clifford's articulate autobiography interrupts a plan because something in it matters to his choice.
- **Dr. Evil / Clifford:** The Vader mistake and retained real lightsaber leave awkward social residue. Dr. Evil overcompensates through references; Clifford's sincere thanks make it harder for him to admit the original error.
- **Dutch / Clifford:** Their different ideas about loyalty and family collide. Dutch tries to narrate a shared past; adult Jack has his own memory and judgment. Genuine Bob grief and Clifford's trauma deserve occasional space without an immediate gag.
- **Dr. Evil / Dutch:** Private competence, public competition over who made the decision. Dutch can understand persuasion better; Dr. Evil can wield actual resources. Neither wins every exchange.
- **Brian / everyone else:** Vary the attacks. Arthur punctures an argument; Chris uses intimate history; Daynuh notices selfish behavior; Billy threatens Brian's inflated importance through scale; Clifford questions his usefulness; Dr. Evil withholds status. Outsiders need not know private nicknames, Busta fixation or sleep incidents before learning them.
- **Arthur / Dutch and Chris / Clifford:** Preserve actual grievances. A useful exchange need not reconcile them. Clifford can remember an injury long after Chris treats it as an old joke; Arthur can recognize a Dutch pitch before anyone else.

### Independent and native cast

- **Kaze / returning K.K.:** Neither owes the other a sentimental reunion performance. Kaze may overprotect; K.K. can find it invasive, funny or offensive. Let the experience of death affect him without making him a different generic innocent.
- **Chris / returning K.K.:** Growing him did not make Chris his owner. Practical care can become an argument over unwanted authority.
- **Starkiller / TPP:** Terms, coercion and respect matter more than immediate team chemistry. His real menace changes how much theatrical bullshit the others dare attempt.
- **Shang Tsung / Dr. Evil:** Two self-promoters test what the other can actually deliver. Let one limited success make the subsequent betrayal credible.
- **Riddick / Arthur:** Conditional mutual respect expressed through practical work and suspicion. Neither needs a long speech about how dangerous the other is.
- **Kratos / Mimir:** Familiar corrections, dry history and practical care. Mimir remains the same physically attached head, not a second narrator.
- **Thor / the older World Serpent:** Recognition and history complicate actual shared-world problems. Every appearance need not replay a fight; one enormous independent choice can change travel.
- **Magneto / Kaze or Noxians:** A principled disagreement with material consequences. He can protect a community without becoming the Sins' hired metal controller or equating all mages with mutants.
- **Frank / Kaze:** Different methods and judgment without manufacturing a fight that violates Kaze's absolute nonattack/protection direction. Trust can include refusing a request.
- **Swain / Darius:** Agreement about the threat, disagreement about the acceptable cost. A short exchange ends in an order that someone must carry out.
- **Darius / Draven:** Family familiarity punctures public persona. Private irritation need not undo loyalty or make either a fool.
- **Other Noxians and wider reserved cast:** Give a reachable return one specific desire and one relationship to change. Riven, Samira, Talon, Katarina, Heisenberg and the optional WTF cast do not all need invitations into the same scene. No presumed arrivals, alliances, current locations or new powers.
