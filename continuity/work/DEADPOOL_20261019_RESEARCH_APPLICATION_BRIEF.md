## Effective narrator identity and boundaries

1. **Only narrator:** the established same post-2024-film Wade narrates ordinary main episodes from E33. He cannot physically turn up from E35 onwards because the narration layer is NOT new in-world body access; E19 physical history stays archived. Billy separately narrates historical campfire Specials. Same Black Mimir is now a physically carried head on Kratos's hip following actual E33 fall/attachment, not a rival narrator or remote intelligence source.
2. **Fourth-wall privilege:** Wade can speak conspiratorially to listeners, notice story framing and cinematic conventions, mock credits/casting/genre, and change emphasis or disclose his own unreliable *interpretations*. Other actors remain immersed; Kaze/Chris/TPP/Kratos/K.K. don't hear his commentary. His awareness does not create objects, change outcomes, repair source defects, reveal sealed plans, manufacture footage, move bodies or confer the audience's knowledge on scene participants.
3. **Truth anchor:** refuse to call a hypothetical plan delivered or a dead character alive; keep locations, elapsed time, exact injuries, body switches, custody, ammunition, witness routes and actor consent factual. Wade's misleading self-description is allowed; fair suspense can hide a name until observed clues support the reveal. A genuine continuity hole isn't repaired by him shouting that writing is lazy.
4. **Rick influence secondary:** surgical insults, irritated cosmic contempt and escalating absurd logic are available. He does not inherit Rick's memories, science, arrogance-as-permanent-competence, burps or central emotional outlook. Wade is hurt, connection-seeking, embarrassed by failure, genuinely capable of loyalty and sometimes almost frightened of being irrelevant.

## What makes Wade's speech recognizable rather than generic swearing

- **Audience conspiracy**: frames the viewer as a slightly shameful friend, not a passive auditorium. His self-serving explanations invite complicity, then collapse under observable truth.
- **False confidence covering exposed need**: a giant title, angry institutional rant, baroque adult metaphor or superhero boast ultimately comes from wanting to matter, to be loved, or to avoid humiliation.
- **Specificity over volume**: target the unique hypocrisy, object, social status or physical absurdity in the scene. Profanity intensity can be extreme but it isn't a filler meter.
- **Fast pivots, short-to-long contrast**: a blunt remark opens a tangent; interruption or self-contradiction makes it stranger; a compact pivot returns to the live action. Maintain an audible subject, location and consequence at each return.
- **Earned self-own**: Wade often gets caught projecting or bluffing and admits it, grudgingly. He isn't a perfect referee able to explain every character's moral motives.
- **Comic structure with aftermath**: the opening 'family story' misdirection in 2018 eventually pays off emotionally. In S7NS, an extended meta joke should meaningfully reframe a relationship, expectation or hypocrisy rather than merely name a studio.
- **Silence is authentic fidelity**: don't explain over Kay Kay's honest admission about killing, Kaze's genuine 'Fair', Kratos's quiet care for Mimir, or real grief if a quieter beat is stronger. Logan's 2024 humiliation of Wade is evidence that the 'merc with a mouth' can become the one who runs out of words.
- **Narration is NOT everybody's dialogue**: Billy's cultured ancient menace, Chris's Theo-derived weirdness, Daynuh's Brok-esque practical style, Daniel's true-drunk ingenuity, Arthur's low frontier wit, Shake's narcissism, Frylock's calm corrections, children's different voices, Dutch's charisma and Tracy's outraged personal identity remain their own.
